What is Space Science?

Space science is the study of everything beyond Earth's atmosphere. It combines physics, chemistry, and biology to explore the cosmos.

Scientists use powerful telescopes and robotic spacecraft to examine planets, stars, and galaxies. By studying these celestial bodies, we can learn how the universe began and how it evolves.

Space science also looks for signs of alien life on distant worlds and helps us understand Earth's place in the vast universe.

Ultimately, this field turns ancient wonders into modern discoveries, showing that we are all deeply connected to the stars above.
