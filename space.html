Space: A Beginner's Guide to the Universe

Welcome to the Space section of SatyaNova.

Have you ever looked at the night sky and wondered what is out there?

In this guide, you will learn the basic building blocks of the universe in simple words.

"Explore the Universe. Discover the Unknown." 🚀✨

What is Space ?

Space is the vast area beyond Earth.

It contains planets, stars, moons, galaxies, dust, gas, and many other objects.

Space is so large that it is difficult for us to imagine its true size.

Most of space is almost empty, but it contains billions of amazing objects.

What is a Star?

A star is a giant ball of hot gas that produces its own light and heat.

Stars shine because of powerful reactions happening inside them.

The closest star to Earth is the Sun.


Examples of Stars
  • Sun
  • Sirius
  • Betelgeuse
  • Polaris (North Star)

Without the Sun, life on Earth would not exist.

What is a Planet?

A planet is a large object that moves around a star.

Unlike stars, planets do not create their own light. They reflect the light of their star.


Examples of Planets
  • Mercury
  • Venus
  • Earth
  • Mars
  • Jupiter
  • Saturn
  • Uranus
  • Neptune

Earth is the only known planet that supports life.

What is a Moon?

A moon is a natural object that moves around a planet.

Many planets have moons.

Example
  • Earth has one moon.
  • Jupiter and Saturn have many moons.

The Moon helps create tides in Earth's oceans.

What is a Star System?

A star system is a group of objects that orbit around a star.

These objects can include:
  • Planets
  • Moons
  • Asteroids
  • Comets

Our Sun and everything that moves around it form a star system.

What is the Solar System?

The Solar System is our home in space.

It includes:
  • The Sun
  • 8 planets
  • More than 200 moons
  • Asteroids
  • Comets
  • Dust and gas

The Sun is at the center of the Solar System.

All planets move around the Sun because of gravity.

The 8 Planets in Order

Mercury → Venus → Earth → Mars → Jupiter → Saturn → Uranus → Neptune

What is an Asteroid?

Asteroids are rocky objects that travel through space.

They are smaller than planets.

Most asteroids are found in the Asteroid Belt between Mars and Jupiter.

What is a Comet?

A comet is a space object made of ice, dust, and rock.

When a comet comes close to the Sun, it forms a bright tail.

Some comets take many years to complete one journey around the Sun.

What is a Galaxy?

A galaxy is a huge collection of stars, planets, gas, dust, and other objects held together by gravity.

Galaxies can contain billions of stars.

Our Galaxy

Our Solar System is located in the Milky Way galaxy.

The Milky Way contains hundreds of billions of stars.
