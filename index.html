What is Science?

Science is a way of learning about the world around us.

It helps us understand how things work by asking questions and finding answers through observation and testing.


Science helps us learn about:
  • The Earth
  • The Sun, Moon, and stars
  • Animals and plants
  • The human body
  • Technology and machines

Scientists do not simply believe something because someone says it. They look for proof and evidence.


Example

If you want to know whether a plant grows better in sunlight or in a dark room, you can test it yourself and see the result.

This is science.


Science asks:

"How does it work?"

What is Spirituality?

Spirituality is about understanding yourself.

It is the journey of knowing your thoughts, feelings, mind, and purpose in life.


Spirituality can help people:
  • Feel calm
  • Understand themselves better
  • Find peace of mind
  • Live with kindness and awareness

Many people practice spirituality through:
  • Meditation
  • Prayer
  • Self-thinking
  • Spending quiet time alone

Example

When you sit quietly and think about your life, your actions, and your feelings, you are exploring yourself.

This is a part of spirituality.


Spirituality asks:

"Who am I?"

Science and Spirituality

Science and spirituality are different, but both help us learn.

Science helps us understand the outside world.

Spirituality helps us understand the inside world.
