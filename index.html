1. What exactly do I get for ₹499?
You get a fully customized website built using one of our sample designs, filled with your content (text, images, services, contact info), and made mobile-friendly. It's a complete, ready-to-share website — not a template you have to build yourself.
2. Do I need any technical knowledge to get a website?
Absolutely not. Our process is 100% beginner-friendly. You simply choose a design, share your details with us, and we handle everything. No coding, no software, no learning curve required.
3. How long does it take to deliver my website?
Most websites are delivered within 3–5 working days after we receive all your content. For simpler projects, it can be even faster. We'll keep you updated throughout the process.
4. Can I see sample designs before ordering?
Yes! We have a collection of sample website designs across different categories — portfolio, blog, business, and more. You choose the one you like and we customize it to match your brand and content.

Depending on your website type, you'll typically need to share: your name and bio, a profile or business photo, your services or skills, contact details, and social media links. We'll send you a simple checklist after you order.

The ₹499 covers website design and development. Hosting and domain (your website address) may be separate, depending on your chosen plan. We'll clearly explain all costs upfront — no surprises.
7. Can I make changes to the website after it's delivered?
Yes — one round of revisions is included in the base price. For ongoing updates or additional changes, we offer affordable update packages. We'll always be transparent about what's included.
8. Is the website mobile-friendly?
Yes, every website we build is fully mobile-responsive. It will look and work great on smartphones, tablets, and desktops. In India, most visitors use mobile — so this is always a priority for us.
9. Which types of websites do you build?
We build Portfolio Websites, Personal Blogs, Business Detail Pages, Student Portfolios, Freelancer Portfolios, and Startup Landing Pages. If you have a specific need not listed, message us on WhatsApp — we're happy to discuss custom projects.
